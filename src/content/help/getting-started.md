LeadOrbiq is a Chrome extension designed to help you collect and organize business lead information while researching prospects, primarily through Google Maps.

## What LeadOrbiq is for

If your work involves finding local businesses in a specific niche and city, reviewing them, and turning that research into an organized list, LeadOrbiq is built around that workflow. It's aimed at local SEO agencies, web design agencies, freelancers, B2B sales teams, lead-generation professionals, and consultants who do this kind of research regularly.

## The basic workflow

1. **Search your target market.** Choose the service, industry, and location you want to research in Google Maps.
2. **Review relevant businesses.** Look through the results and focus on companies that match your campaign.
3. **Collect and organize details.** Use LeadOrbiq to reduce repetitive copying and keep prospect information in a more consistent structure.
4. **Continue your outreach workflow.** Review your list, verify important details, and use the information to create relevant, personalized outreach.
