If something isn't working the way you expect, these general checks resolve many extension issues before you need to reach out for support.

## The extension icon isn't visible

Chrome sometimes hides new extensions behind the puzzle-piece menu in the toolbar. Click the puzzle-piece icon and pin LeadOrbiq so it stays visible.

## Nothing happens when you open the panel

Try reloading the Google Maps tab. Browser extensions that interact with a page's content sometimes need the page to load fresh after installation or an update.

## The extension seems out of date

Chrome usually updates extensions automatically, but you can check manually from `chrome://extensions`. Make sure Developer Mode's auto-update behavior hasn't been disabled.

## You're seeing unexpected results

Since LeadOrbiq works alongside Google Maps' own search and results, anything unusual in the underlying search results (like Google Maps layout changes) can affect what the extension sees. If this keeps happening, let us know through the [Contact page](/contact/) with details about what you searched and what you expected.

## Still stuck?

Reach out through the [Contact page](/contact/) and choose "Product support" as the topic. Include your Chrome version and a description of what you were doing when the issue occurred — that context makes it much faster to help.
