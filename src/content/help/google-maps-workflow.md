LeadOrbiq is positioned primarily around local-business prospecting through Google Maps. It's meant to sit alongside the research you're already doing there, not replace it.

## Starting a search

The workflow starts the same way most local-business research does: searching Google Maps for a service and location, for example "plumbers in Denver, CO." From there, you review the results the way you normally would.

## Reviewing results

Not every result in a Google Maps search is a good prospect. Part of the workflow is reviewing listings and deciding which businesses actually match your campaign — the right industry, the right location, the right size, or whatever criteria matter for your outreach. Advanced filters help you narrow results down before you start reviewing.

## Automatic map expansion

Some niches only have a handful of businesses in a given area. When LeadOrbiq detects that a niche is exhausted nearby, it automatically expands the map area so you keep finding new businesses instead of manually re-running the search farther out.

## Where LeadOrbiq fits in

As you review businesses you want to keep track of, LeadOrbiq is designed to help you collect and organize that information instead of manually copying it into a separate spreadsheet or document. See [Collecting and organizing leads](/help/collecting-leads/) for more on that step.

## Exact compatibility

The precise supported Google Maps workflows — including pagination behavior and any auto-scroll functionality — should match the current extension release. Check the Chrome Web Store listing for the most current description of supported behavior.
