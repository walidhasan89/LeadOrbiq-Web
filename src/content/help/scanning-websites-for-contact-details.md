Business name and category get you started, but outreach usually needs more — an email address, a phone number, a social profile. Scan Website fills that in automatically.

## How it works

Once you've collected a business with a website, click **Scan Website** on that lead. LeadOrbiq's AI reads the site and pulls out:

- **Email address**
- **Phone number**
- **Social media links**

The scan runs quickly, so you can move through a list of collected leads without opening and searching each site by hand.

## What it doesn't do

Scan Website reads publicly available information on the business's own site. It doesn't guess, doesn't fabricate contact details that aren't published, and doesn't visit anything beyond the business's website.

## Use the results responsibly

Details pulled by Scan Website should still be verified before you rely on them for outreach, and any contact you make should follow applicable marketing and privacy laws — see [How LeadOrbiq handles data](/help/data-handling-and-privacy/) for more.
