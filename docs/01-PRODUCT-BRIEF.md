# LeadOrbiq Product Brief

## Product type

LeadOrbiq is a **Chrome extension**.

## Core purpose

LeadOrbiq helps users collect and organize useful business lead information while researching prospects, primarily through Google Maps.

## Simple positioning

**Turn Google Maps research into organized prospect lists.**

## Longer positioning

LeadOrbiq helps agencies, freelancers, sales teams, and business owners reduce repetitive prospecting work by collecting and organizing local-business information in a cleaner workflow.

## Primary audience

- Local SEO agencies
- Web design and development agencies
- Freelancers
- Lead-generation professionals
- B2B sales teams
- Appointment setters
- Recruiters researching local businesses
- Consultants
- Home-service marketing teams
- Business owners doing direct outreach

## Primary jobs to be done

- Find local businesses in a target niche and city
- Review prospects efficiently
- Collect useful company details
- Build an organized lead list
- Prepare data for personalized outreach
- Reduce copying and pasting
- Avoid disorganized spreadsheets
- Move from discovery to outreach faster

## Main pain points

- Manually copying each company name and contact detail
- Repeating the same Google Maps research
- Losing track of which prospects were reviewed
- Cleaning inconsistent spreadsheet data
- Switching between multiple tabs and tools
- Spending too much time preparing outreach lists
- Collecting leads without a clear workflow
- Duplicating previously gathered prospects

## Desired outcomes

- Faster research
- Cleaner prospect data
- Better organization
- More consistent lead collection
- Easier handoff to outreach workflows
- Less repetitive data entry
- More time for personalized messaging

- Provide appropriate opt-out methods
